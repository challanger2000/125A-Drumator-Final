125A DRUMATOR - KIT LIBRARY

Place authentic drumkits as .125akit files in this directory.
In the plugin, click KITS, select a drumkit and press LOAD.
Only sample assets, names and per-sample trims are imported.
Your existing patterns, grooves, song chain and mixer settings are preserved.
Empty instrument slots remain silent. No old automatic/factory drumkits are included.
Kit files must be encoded using the verified KitCodec format; renaming a .vstpreset will not convert it.
