# Drumator V1.0.1 — GUI/UX redesign specification (draft 01)

Status: **DESIGN IN PROGRESS — NOT APPROVED FOR VSTGUI IMPLEMENTATION**.
Date: 2026-10-27. Branch: `develop/v1.0.1-gui-redesign`.
Starting baseline: Final repository commit `e84df4ea8c317e12bf792a0a2396c810ed2d6f02`.
V1.0.0 main, binary, and packaging are not to be modified or reclassified as released.

## Product concept

**FL Studio-style multi-row step sequencer within the housing of a high-quality hardware drum machine.**
The 32 step controls (single continuous horizontal line per instrument), their dark-blue wells, orange on-state, and collapsible per-row velocity lane are the distinguishing strengths and **must be preserved**.
Roland TR-808/909, D16 Drumazon 2, Sugar Bytes DrumComputer, Arturia Spark 2 and XLN XO are UX/industrial design references, not material or code to copy.

## Owner decisions already made

- Entire plugin must be operable through ordinary mouse interactions. Shift/Ctrl/Alt gestures may remain optional, never sole access.
- Always-visible: actions used frequently during sequencing. Secondary operations may reside in obvious, mouse-accessible contextual menus or expanding sections.
- Hardware-like, disciplined layout: repeated controls form a fixed sizing system. Text length must not determine per-instance button width. Consistent alignments/gutters and limited button families.
- Labels may be abbreviated when context makes them clear; document abbreviations in the manual. `PTNR` preferred over `PATTERN MENU`.
- Minimum necessary width per component, with adequate hit targets and legibility at 100/120/130/150% zoom. Do not fill all newly freed space.
- Keep header controls on one horizontal band rather than inventing additional stacked control rows.
- Consider a matte cream-white instrument faceplate and black hardware buttons, but do not recolor the dark pattern background/steps. Color/material choice remains **PROPOSED**, not approved final.
- Preserve the existing sound engine, automation/state IDs, source/asset loading, host and song functionality.

## Audited GUI code: src/vst3/DrumatorView.cpp (baseline)

Instrument row around lines 1162–1268:
- Instrument number selects the row.
- Sample-name click loads file, Shift+click renames row, Alt+click clears sample.

- CHOKE selector: groups 0–4.

- RANDOM VEL action at 0% legacy random realtime amount; at nonzero, legacy RANDOM RT rotary.
- 32 individual steps and expandable VEL lane.

Header around lines 1015–1095:
- Pattern length: 1 BAR or 2 BARS buttons for the current pattern (16 or 32 steps).
- Eight direct A–H buttons; Shift+click on A–H is an optional length shortcut.
- PATTERN MENU: COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, UNDO, RANDOM VELOCITY, VELOCITY 100, DUPLICATE.
- SONG ON/OFF; SONG EDIT/PATTERN view switch.
- SWING / HUMANIZE / MASTER rotaries; MAIN L/R output meters + clip; zoom selection.

## Proposed interaction map (to be approved before coding)

1. Sample selection field comprises filename + integrated previous/next arrows; direct mouse click to load remains.
2. Adjacent small Preview (triangle), EDIT, and visible `...` sample-menu entry.
3. M and S in a fixed shared button family. Group VOL, PAN, PITCH symmetrically; avoid variable-width adjacent controls.
4. Keep step well **exactly in one 32-step row** and the current VEL toggle/lane.
5. Consolidate ROW commands via contextual ROW menu close to the row identity; place routing/choke in a discoverable secondary control section if owner agrees.
6. Keep A–H and compact `PTNR` menu next to each other; one compact 1/2 BAR chooser; song and global controls in same horizontal head band.
7. Never hide necessary sample rename/remove, knob reset, or pattern length behind mandatory keyboard modifiers.

## Engineering release gates
